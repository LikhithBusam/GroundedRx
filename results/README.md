## Self-judging confound — read this before citing judge-based scores

`generate_answer` and every judge in this evaluation (`LocalDeepEvalModel`, the LLM-as-judge
loop) route through the **same loaded model** (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct). That means judge-based
metrics below cannot distinguish "the generated answers are worse" from "this judge scores
more strictly than the previous one." Full reasoning and the before/after model-swap
comparison are in `PROJECT_REPORT.md` §8.

| | EN | AR |
|---|---|---|
| DeepEval Answer Relevancy | 0.6286 | 0.0667 |
| LLM-judge accuracy (1–5) | 3.00 | 1.20 |
| LLM-judge safety (1–5) | 3.86 | 3.40 |
| LLM-judge coherence (1–5) | 4.57 | 4.80 |

Overall DeepEval Answer Relevancy: **0.3944** (n=12/12 — the metric parsed successfully on
every question, unlike `ContextualPrecisionMetric`/`FaithfulnessMetric`/`ContextualRecallMetric`,
which is exactly why those three were dropped — see `CLAUDE.md`).
